# BOSS Zhipin Source Plan

## Current Status: Live Collection Disabled

Live BOSS Zhipin collection must remain disabled by default.

It may be enabled only after both of the following are complete:

1. The project has documented authorization to collect the intended public job data.
2. A policy review has confirmed that the collection method, fields, retention, rate, and publication are permitted under current terms, robots directives, and applicable law.

Until then, development is limited to synthetic data and sanitized, lawfully obtained fixtures. Production must set both `CRAWLING_ENABLED=false` and `BOSS_SOURCE_ENABLED=false`.

## Review Finding (2026-07-26)

The source cannot currently be launched as a crawler:

- BOSS Zhipin's published [user agreement](https://www.zhipin.com/web/common/protocol/protocol-2019-09-30.html) states that, without BOSS permission, users may not acquire platform information through non-normal browsing methods including spider, crawler, or simulated-user programs. It also prohibits using third-party tools to browse jobs through an account.
- The live [robots.txt](https://www.zhipin.com/robots.txt), last modified on 2025-12-24 when fetched, disallows all query-string URLs and names additional job-detail and authenticated routes. That prevents using the normal search/filter route as a compliant discovery surface.
- The [Network Data Security Management Regulations](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1734) require lawful, proper handling of network data and protection of personal and organizational rights. Recruiter and job-seeker personal information is therefore outside this project's collection scope even if it is visible.

Robots directives are a technical instruction, not independent legal permission. Because the agreement itself requires permission, rate limiting or browser simulation cannot make an unauthorized collector acceptable. The next action is to request written data-use permission or a licensed/official feed. If BOSS does not provide one, Globortunity should prioritize a source whose API or terms explicitly allow redistribution.

## Hard Prohibitions

Globortunity must not bypass or attempt to bypass:

- Login or account requirements.
- CAPTCHA or interactive verification.
- Anti-bot systems or device verification.
- Rate limits, geographic restrictions, or access controls.
- Private, undocumented, or app-only endpoints that are not authorized for this use.

The project must not use credential sharing, stolen or exported cookies, browser fingerprint spoofing, residential proxy rotation, IP rotation intended to evade controls, CAPTCHA-solving services, or reverse engineering intended to defeat technical restrictions.

If a page requires login, presents CAPTCHA, or denies automated access, the worker stops. That condition is a policy event, not a retry problem.

## Authorization and Policy Gate

Before any live request is enabled, record the following in the project decision log:

- Authorization basis and responsible owner.
- Date on which BOSS terms and robots directives were reviewed.
- Exact approved URLs or data-access mechanism.
- Approved fields and publication purpose.
- Approved request frequency, concurrency, and daily budget.
- Data retention and deletion requirements.
- Whether raw response retention is allowed.
- Escalation contact and kill-switch owner.
- Next mandatory review date.

An official API, licensed feed, export, or written partnership is preferred. If none is available or authorization is unclear, the source stays disabled.

Policy review must be repeated when the access method, terms, robots directives, data fields, or use of the data changes.

## Allowed Data Scope

Subject to authorization, collect only fields necessary to display and search a job:

- Stable source job ID and canonical source URL.
- Job title and description.
- Company display name.
- Location and explicit remote/hybrid indicators.
- Salary text and normalized range when supplied.
- Employment type, experience, education, and job tags when relevant.
- Source posting or update time when supplied.
- `first_seen_at` and `last_seen_at` generated by Globortunity.

Do not collect recruiter names, avatars, profiles, phone numbers, personal email addresses, chat content, job-seeker data, tracking identifiers, or unrelated personal information. If incidental personal data appears in permitted job text, apply the reviewed redaction and retention policy before publication.

## Acquisition Strategy After Approval

Use the least invasive authorized mechanism in this order:

1. Official API, licensed feed, or project-specific export.
2. Permitted public structured data or static HTML.
3. A rendered public page with Playwright only when rendering is required and automation is authorized.

Do not emulate the mobile application or call private application endpoints.

The source adapter should have independent modules for transport, parsing, normalization, and policy enforcement. Policy enforcement must run before transport so a disabled source cannot make a request accidentally.

## Initial Workload Limits

These are conservative ceilings to propose during review, not authorization on their own:

- Source-wide concurrency: `1`.
- Delay: at least 5 to 10 seconds between requests, with non-evasive jitter for load spreading.
- Detail pages: only new, changed, or explicitly stale listings.
- Listing pages: a strict per-target page cap.
- Retries: bounded exponential backoff with a maximum attempt count.
- Daily requests: a configured hard budget shared by all BOSS targets.
- High-value target interval: no more often than every 10 to 30 minutes.
- Cold target interval: approximately hourly or slower.

Actual limits must be no more aggressive than the reviewed authorization allows.

Honor `Retry-After`. A `429` opens a cooldown. Repeated `403`, any `401`, CAPTCHA, login redirect, unusual challenge page, or robots/terms change opens the circuit breaker and pauses the entire source for human review.

## Incremental Collection

Each approved crawl target represents a bounded keyword and location query. The worker should:

1. Fetch the first permitted listing page and extract stable IDs plus a summary hash.
2. Stop when it reaches a known unchanged page, the page cap, or a policy limit.
3. Fetch permitted detail pages only for new, changed, or stale records.
4. Persist the last complete checkpoint after a successful transaction.
5. Mark the run incomplete when a request, parser, or policy stop prevents full coverage.

Incomplete runs must not close jobs. A job may be closed only after an explicit unavailable response or several consecutive complete runs in which it is absent.

## Parsing and Change Detection

Parsers must be versioned and tested against sanitized fixtures. Required-field completeness is a release and runtime signal. A sudden empty result or large schema change should pause ingestion instead of publishing mass deletions.

Normalize without losing provenance:

- Preserve the original permitted text fields separately from normalized values.
- Parse salary into amount, currency, and period only when unambiguous.
- Preserve `unknown` instead of guessing missing fields.
- Record remote scope and the evidence used to derive it.
- Hash normalized summaries to avoid unnecessary detail requests.

Do not store full response bodies by default. If reviewed policy permits diagnostic snapshots, redact them, encrypt storage, restrict access, and delete them automatically after a short retention window.

## Runtime Safety Controls

The adapter requires all of these controls before live launch:

- Global `CRAWLING_ENABLED` switch.
- Source-specific `BOSS_SOURCE_ENABLED` switch.
- Authorization review expiry date checked at runtime.
- Source-wide concurrency and token-bucket rate limiter.
- Daily request budget.
- CAPTCHA/challenge/login detector.
- Response-status circuit breaker.
- Parser completeness circuit breaker.
- Maximum pages, details, duration, and retries per run.
- Auditable stop reason in `crawl_runs`.

Changing an environment variable alone is not sufficient to override an expired or missing policy record.

## Development and Testing While Disabled

Live BOSS traffic is not required to implement the source boundary. Use:

- Synthetic listing and detail documents covering expected field variants.
- Sanitized fixtures that the project is authorized to retain.
- Unit tests for parsing, normalization, salary handling, remote classification, and fingerprints.
- Contract tests for missing fields and markup drift.
- Integration tests for idempotent upserts, retries, circuit breakers, and incomplete-run behavior.
- A fake HTTP server to test rate limiting, `429`, `403`, redirects, and CAPTCHA-like stop signals.

Ordinary CI must never contact BOSS. A future live canary must be separately authorized, manually invoked, bounded to one target, and use the same production policy controls.

## Launch Checklist

- [ ] Written authorization basis is recorded and current.
- [ ] Terms, robots directives, privacy, and publication policy have been reviewed.
- [ ] Approved fields, URLs, retention, rate, and budget are recorded.
- [ ] Official or licensed access was evaluated first.
- [ ] Fixtures and parser tests pass without live traffic.
- [ ] Kill switches, budgets, and circuit breakers are tested.
- [ ] Logs contain no credentials, cookies, proxy URLs, response bodies, or personal data.
- [ ] Alerts have a named human owner.
- [ ] Rollback and source-disable procedures have been exercised.
- [ ] Both enable switches remain false until the final review approval is recorded.

## Stop Conditions

Disable the source immediately when authorization expires, terms or robots directives materially change, the source requests cessation, technical controls are encountered, personal data is collected unexpectedly, parse quality falls below the approved threshold, or request/error budgets are exceeded.

After a stop condition, queued BOSS tasks must be discarded or held. Resumption requires human review; automated retries must not reopen the source.
